# Misty Lake — Common (8). Source: Wikipedia (FishBase/IGFA cross-check still to do)

## Bluegill (Lepomis macrochirus)
- Typical 10-30 cm; max ~41 cm / 2.0 kg; IGFA 2.15 kg (1950, Alabama)
- Deep, flattened body, small terminal mouth, rough scales
- Olive back with 5-9 dark bars; blue/purple face and chin; orange-yellow belly
- Black "ear" flap on gill cover; dark blotch at rear base of dorsal fin
- Notched tail, large flexible pectorals. Fight: fast bursts, sharp turns (source does not describe angling fight)

## Pumpkinseed (Lepomis gibbosus) -> game name "Reed Sunfish"
- Typical ~10 cm, up to 28 cm; usually < 450 g; record 680 g (2016, New York)
- Compact oval ("pumpkin seed") body, small mouth
- Speckled orange/green/yellow/blue; 7-8 faint vertical bars; yellow-orange belly; blue lines on cheeks
- Black gill flap with orange-red spot on its edge; orange spots on dorsal/anal/tail; black dorsal spines
- Fight: "impressively vigorous" for its size

## Yellow Perch (Perca flavescens)
- Typical 10-25 cm (avg 19 cm); max 50 cm / 1.9 kg (1865, New Jersey)
- Elongate, laterally compressed, blunt snout, TWO separate dorsal fins, forked tail
- Green-olive-golden back, yellow flanks, white/yellow belly; 6-8 dark vertical bars
- Black blotch on first dorsal fin; fins yellow to green, lower fins yellow-silver (red/yellow on spawning males)
- Fight: weak swimmer, schools (source) — suggest low surge for common tier

## Black Crappie (Pomoxis nigromaculatus)
- Typical 10-20 cm; max 49 cm / ~2.7 kg; IGFA 2.47 kg
- Deep, flattened; large mouth reaching below the eye, thin lips
- Silvery-gray to green with irregular mottled black splotches; spotted rows on dorsal/anal/tail
- 7-8 dorsal spines (key ID); dorsal and anal fins similar, large and rounded

## Rock Bass (Ambloplites rupestris)
- Typical 15-25 cm; max 43 cm / 1.4 kg; IGFA 1.36 kg
- Looks like a small stocky smallmouth; large mouth
- Golden brown to olive back, white/silver belly, RED eyes; rows of dark dots along sides
- Two dorsal fins joined; 6 anal spines (others usually 3)

## Brown Bullhead (Ameiurus nebulosus)
- Typical 20-50 cm, most < 1 kg; max 53 cm / 3.6 kg; IGFA 3.3 kg
- Stout, SCALELESS body; dark brown-green back fading to yellow-green sides, cream belly; dark brown-black speckles
- 8 barbels (chin barbels black to yellowish-brown); slightly subterminal mouth
- Tail only slightly notched; one small adipose fin; stiff dorsal spine and serrated pectoral spines
- Fight: spirited but not especially strong; bottom feeder

## White Sucker (Catostomus commersonii)
- Typical 30-51 cm, 0.9-2.7 kg; IGFA 2.9 kg (1984, Rainy River, Minnesota)
- Long, cylindrical body; underslung sucker mouth with thick fleshy lips
- Dark green/gray/copper/brown/black back and sides, pale underside; forked tail

## Golden Shiner (Notemigonus crysoleucas)
- Typical 7-20 cm; max 36.7 cm
- Laterally compressed, deep body, small UPTURNED mouth; lateral line curves sharply down
- Fleshy keel between pelvic and anal fin; large anal fin (8-19 rays)
- Dark olive back, brassy-white belly, silver or golden flanks, yellow-green eyes and fins; faint dusky side stripe
