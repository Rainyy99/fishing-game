## Muskellunge (Esox masquinongy)
- Typical 70-120 cm, 7-16.5 kg; up to ~1.8 m / ~30 kg; IGFA 30.5 kg (1949, Wisconsin), 153 cm
- Elongated torpedo body, FLAT head; dorsal, pelvic and anal fins set far back; tail lobes more pointed than pike
- Light silver/brown/green base; dark VERTICAL stripes that may break into spots (pike has light spots on dark; muskie is the reverse)
- Variants: Great Lakes (oblique spot rows), Chautauqua (barred), clear (few/no markings)
- No scales on lower half of gill covers. Fight: not in source (explosive ambush strike)

## Lake Trout (Salvelinus namaycush) -> game name "Crystal Trout"
- Average 61-91 cm, 6.8-18 kg not uncommon; rod record 33 kg / 150 cm (Great Bear Lake, 1995)
- Elongated salmonid, largest char; lean form slimmer, siscowet form fatter
- Uniform olive-grey back, white belly; light beige/white SPOTS; yellowish fins sometimes white-tipped; no spines
- Spawning: belly yellow-orange, fins reddish with prominent white edges
- Deeply forked tail is general knowledge, not in source (verify). Fight: long fights stress fish

## Flathead Catfish (Pylodictis olivaris)
- Usually 38-114 cm; IGFA 55.79 kg (123 lb, Kansas 1998), 155 cm
- Small eyes; LOWER JAW projects past upper jaw; wide flat head (general knowledge, verify)
- Olive back/sides, white-to-yellow underside; young fish heavily MOTTLED dark brown-black, mottling may extend into fins
- Tail shallowly notched (emarginate), upper lobe may have an isolated white edge; anal fin rounded (14-17 rays); fins mostly brown
- Barbels not in source (all catfish have them; verify count). Fight: not in source

## Bowfin (Amia calva)
- Typical ~50 cm; females 65-70 cm, males 50-65 cm; max 109 cm / 9.75 kg; tackle record 9.8 kg
- Long cylindrical streamlined body, rounded head, smooth scale-free head, BONY GULAR PLATE under the jaw, sharp teeth
- Olive-brown with vertical bars and dark mottling, white/cream underside; paired and anal fins BRIGHT GREEN
- VERY LONG undulating dorsal fin (145-250 rays) from mid-back to tail base, with horizontal bars
- Males: black EYESPOT at tail base ringed orange-yellow. Tail rounded, barred
- Fight: strong, agile game fish

## Longnose Gar (Lepisosteus osseus)
- Typical 71-122 cm; max ~1.8 m / 25 kg
- Torpedo-shaped, armored cylindrical body; needle-like snout about 3x head length, row of cone-shaped teeth on each side of upper jaw
- Olive brown to green; thick bone-like rhomboid GANOID scales (diamond-pattern look)
- Fin positions/spots not in source: general knowledge = dorsal and anal fins far back near the tail, dark spots on fins (verify)
