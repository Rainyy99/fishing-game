## Lake Sturgeon (Acipenser fulvescens; page lists it as Huso fulvescens)
- Max ~2.2 m / 108 kg (240 lb, Lake Winnebago female, 87.5 in); typical size not in source
- Streamlined, partly cartilaginous; ROWS OF BONY PLATES (scutes) along sides and back
- Elongated spade-like snout, FOUR barbels near the mouth, rubbery toothless prehensile lips (underslung)
- Colour/fins/tail not in source. General knowledge (verify): olive-brown to grey back, pale belly, heterocercal tail (upper lobe longer), shark-like
- Fight: not in source (heavy bottom fish)

## Paddlefish (Polyodon spathula)
- Typical ~1.5 m / 27 kg; largest 2.16 m (1916 Iowa); rod-and-reel record 74.8 kg at 1.58 m
- Shark-like tapering body, cartilaginous; bluish-grey to black on top, mottled, whitish belly; SCALELESS smooth skin
- Flat PADDLE rostrum about 1/3 of body length; large toothless mouth; large tapering gill flap
- Tail deeply forked with the spine extending into the upper lobe (shark-like)
- Filter feeder: will NOT take bait (must be snagged) — lore/flavour note for the game, e.g. only a special bait or event

## American Eel (Anguilla rostrata) -> game name "Lake Dragon Eel"
- Max ~1.22 m / 7.5 kg (largest reported, not a confirmed record); females larger, lighter colour, smaller eyes, higher fins
- Long slender snake-like body, slimy smooth skin (tiny scales)
- Yellow eel (immature): olive green/brown/greenish-yellow, light grey-white belly. Silver eel (migrating): grey back, whitish-cream belly
- One long continuous dorsal fin joining anal fin and a small rudimentary tail fin; NO pelvic fins; small pectoral fins behind the head
- Terminal mouth, thick lips, small teeth, small well-developed eyes
- For "Dragon" flavour: could use silver-eel palette with a faint glow or a bigger whiskery head, but keep eel anatomy
