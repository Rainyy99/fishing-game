# Misty Lake — Uncommon (7). Source: Wikipedia (FishBase/IGFA cross-check still to do)

## Largemouth Bass (Micropterus salmoides) -> game name "Lake Bass"
- Max 75 cm / unofficial 11.4 kg; IGFA ~10.1 kg (22 lb 5 oz). Females larger. Typical size not given by source
- Olive green to greenish-gray; jagged horizontal band of dark blotches along each flank
- Upper jaw extends PAST the rear edge of the eye (the "large mouth")
- Fight: very vigorous, leaps to shake the hook

## Smallmouth Bass (Micropterus dolomieu)
- Males avg ~0.9 kg, females 1.4-2.7 kg; max 69 cm / 5.4 kg; IGFA 5.41 kg (1955, Dale Hollow)
- Slender, muscular, torpedo-like (river form long and slim; lake form shorter, deeper, oval)
- Golden-olive to dark brown back, yellowish-white belly; dark vertical bars/blotches on body, dark horizontal bars on the head; red or brown eyes
- Jaw does NOT extend past the eye; two dorsal fins separated by a shallow notch (9-11 spines / 13-15 soft rays)
- Fight: among the toughest freshwater fish in N. America; strong energetic runs

## White Bass (Morone chrysops)
- Common 30-38 cm (up to 43+ cm); IGFA 3.09 kg
- Deep, laterally compressed; notched tail with pointed lobes
- Silver-white to pale green, dark back, white sides/belly; narrow dark LENGTHWISE stripes; large rough scales
- Two separate dorsal fins (front spiny, rear soft)

## Chain Pickerel (Esox niger)
- Typical ~61 cm / 1.5 kg; IGFA 4.25 kg (1961, Georgia); length record 65 cm
- Elongated, pike-like; greenish sides with dark CHAIN-LIKE pattern; vertical dark bar under the eye
- Fully scaled cheeks and gill covers (unlike northern pike); sharp teeth
- Fight: energetic; swallows lures whole

## Rainbow Trout (Oncorhynchus mykiss)
- Stream adults 0.5-2.5 kg; lake/steelhead up to ~9 kg; IGFA 22 kg (triploid, 2009) - ignore as outlier
- Tail squarish, only mildly forked
- Stream: blue-green/olive with heavy black spots; broad reddish-pink stripe along the lateral line (brightest in breeding males)
- Lake form: silvery, red stripe almost gone; juveniles have dark parr marks
- Fight: often called the hardest-fighting trout; leaps, powerful struggle

## Brown Trout (Salmo trutta)
- River fish ~1 kg or less; lake morph 40-80 cm, max ~140 cm; heaviest 20.1 kg (NZ 2020); length record 97 cm
- Slender body, long narrow head, large mouth; squared-off unforked tail
- Silver to brassy reddish-brown, creamy/white belly; dark AND red spots, medium spots with lighter HALOS; spots usually not on the tail
- Variants: Loch Leven (heavy black spots, no red), continental (golden cast)
- Fight: long runs, hard head shakes (1897 angler account)

## Common Carp (Cyprinus carpio)
- Typical 40-80 cm, 2-14 kg; up to 120 cm / 40+ kg; largest 45.59 kg (France 2013)
- Robust, deep body; dark gold sheen (brightest on head); caudal/anal fins bronze with orange tint
- Two pairs of barbels (upper lip corner + lower lip); large conspicuous scales (mirror carp: few large scales; leather: almost none)
- Long tapering dorsal fin over last two-thirds of the body, first spine serrated
